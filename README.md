# The World in One Day — 26 November 2026

[![make.fun](https://make.fun/badge/news-image-20261126.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261126)

A one-page newspaper front page (1024x1024 PNG) summing up the world
news of Thursday 26 November 2026, drawn entirely in code — pure
Python 3 standard library, no network, no external images.

## The day's stories

- **Lead — Gulf oil shock:** Houthis claim a strike on Saudi Arabia's
  Ras Tanura refinery; Riyadh says it shot down four drones. Brent
  crude jumps 3.5% to $94.61 a barrel as tankers are hit by mines in
  the Strait of Hormuz.
- **Trade — Washington:** The US proposes new tariffs on 14 trading
  partners to fight factory overcapacity; Taiwan faces up to 15%.
  Stock futures slide.
- **Gaza — ceasefire under strain:** Israel kills Hamas security chief
  Abuelouf in a Gaza strike; mediators urge calm as the aid crisis
  deepens.
- **Ukraine — Russia:** Ukraine strikes two Russian oil refineries
  overnight; Putin vows retaliation.
- **Space — Asia:** China picks Pakistan's Muhammad Zeeshan Ali for
  Shenzhou-24 — the first foreign astronaut bound for its Tiangong
  space station.
- **Also today:** India marks 18 years since the 26/11 Mumbai attacks;
  Thanksgiving in the US as turkey hits a record $2 a pound; the
  Solomon Islands and Australia sign a security treaty.

Sources: Reuters, Observer Voice, SimpleLife.news, OilPrice — all
stories reported 26 November 2026.

## Run

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

Writes `out/entry.png` (1024x1024, under 1 MiB). Deterministic: two
runs produce byte-identical output.
